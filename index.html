Empathy Map

Understanding players’ behaviors, motivations, and challenges to improve their puzzle experience.

👣DOES

  • Plays during breaks or downtime.
  • Returns for daily puzzles.
  • Tracks scores and streaks.
  • Shares results with friends.

😣PAINS

  • Too many ads or interruptions.
  • Confusing rules or controls.
  • Repetitive puzzles and difficulty spikes.
  • Losing progress or streaks.

🧠THINKS

  • “I want a quick mental workout.”
  • “Can I solve this without hints?”
  • “I want to improve my score.”
  • “I need something fun during a break.”

❤️FEELS

  • Satisfied after solving a puzzle.
  • Curious about new challenges.
  • Frustrated by difficult levels.
  • Disappointed when a streak breaks.

💬SAYS

  • “Let me try one more.”
  • “That was harder than expected.”
  • “I got it!”
  • “I want to share my result.”

✨GAINS

  • A satisfying sense of achievement.
  • Better vocabulary and problem-solving skills.
  • Visible progress and personal bests.
  • Fresh challenges and enjoyable daily routines.
